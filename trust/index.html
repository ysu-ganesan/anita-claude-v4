What we promise, and how you can check it.

Trust

An adjective is easy to print. So here are the promises themselves, each with the way to check it. Where something is not built yet, it says so.

If you talk to Curl

What she keeps is yours.

01

Everything she knows about you is on one page.

How to check it

In the app: Us, then Her memory. Read it and search it there.

02

You can erase any line, or all of it.

How to check it

Erase a line on that page. Delete your account and it all goes.

03

You can take a copy.

How to check it

Us, then Her memory, then the ⋯ button, then Export my memory. You get what she knows, her map, her diary and her dreams as plain text files.

04

It is never sold and never used for ads.

How to check it

It is written in the privacy policy, the document we answer to.

05

She asks who writes her replies before your first talk.

How to check it

Say yes, and an outside AI model writes them. Or keep it Private, and only our own model answers. A video call uses the outside model, in Private too, and asks you first.

06

Your health data is never sent to any AI company.

How to check it

The same sentence is on the Apple Health permission screen and in the privacy policy.

07

She is an AI, and she says so.

How to check it

Ask her.

If we work inside your company

People decide. The data stays in.

01

It runs on your own machines.

How to check it

Drawings, prices and people’s names stay in the building.

02

AI never signs.

How to check it

Every quote, report and approval is drafted by an agent and signed by a person.

03

Every step is logged.

How to check it

The log sits on your own machines, with everything else.

04

Nothing we write moves a real robot before three checks.

How to check it

Your real settings are in, the program runs clean in the robot maker’s own simulator, and a person watches a dry run.

If you build on Nenai

Forgetting is a call, not a support ticket.

Nenai is not open yet. This is how the draft API works today.

01

A forgotten memory leaves search at once.

How to check it

One call forgets it. For 30 days it can be brought back.

02

One call erases everything about one user.

How to check it

For the day one of your users asks you to delete their data.

03

Memory text can be kept away from outside models.

How to check it

Switch the project to local only.

04

Your key stays on your server.

How to check it

The JavaScript client refuses to start in a browser.

On this website

It does very little, on purpose.

01

No cookies, no trackers, no adverts.

How to check it

Open your browser’s storage inspector on any page. It is empty.

02

Nothing is loaded from another company’s servers.

How to check it

The fonts, pictures and scripts all come from this address.

03

The contact form sends only what you type.

How to check it

The page says, before you press the button, exactly how your message will travel.
