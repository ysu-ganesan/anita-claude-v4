Why the brain first

The hard part of a home robot is not the hands.

  1. 01 / 03

    It is knowing the people it lives with. Hands can be bought. Knowing someone takes time, and it cannot be hurried on the day the robot arrives.

  2. 02 / 03

    So the brain learns now, on the phone you already carry. When a body is ready, the idea is that it moves in with everything it knows.

  3. 03 / 03

    The same brain earns its keep in the meantime. It drafts the paperwork in a company’s office, and we are designing the robot cells that will put it to work on the factory floor.
