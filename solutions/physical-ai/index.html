Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

01

Is any of this running in a factory today?

No. The sack-emptying cell is being designed in simulation, truck unloading is being scoped, and the rest is planned. We would rather say so than show a demo as a product.

02

Why practise in simulation first?

A mistake in a computer costs nothing. A mistake on your floor costs scrap or a stopped line. So every move is tried in simulation before a robot makes it for real.

03

Where do we start?

With one job and one honest look at it. Tell us what is lifted, checked or unloaded by hand, and we will say whether a camera and a robot can take it.
