How we come to work

Four terms, the same for everyone.

  • It runs on your own machines, so drawings, prices and people’s names stay in the building.

  • Every quote, report and approval is drafted by an agent and signed by a person. AI never signs, and every step is logged.

  • We agree the success numbers on day one and measure them on day 30. If it does not clear the bar, you keep the report.

  • Designed so that when someone moves on, what they knew stays with the job.
